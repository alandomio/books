# Chapter 13: Embracing the Vibe - Future of Software Engineering

## Overview
This is the conclusion. We step back from the code and look at the trajectory of the industry.
"Vibe Coding" is not just a trend; it's a fundamental shift in how humans interact with machines.
We will synthesize the lessons of the book and offer a vision for the next decade.

## Key Concepts
- **The Abstraction Layer**: AI is the new compiler.
- **The Hollow Engineer**: The danger of forgetting how things work.
- **The New Trinity**: Intent (User), Context (Engineer), Execution (AI).
- **The Manifesto**: A call to action for high-agency engineering.
