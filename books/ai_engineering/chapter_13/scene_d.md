# Scene D: Conclusion

We stood at the start of this book with a blank prompt. We end it with a system of agents that can read, think, code, test, and deploy.

This is the power of the Vibe Coding Era.
But power requires principle.

As we conclude, I offer you this **Manifesto**. A set of guiding principles for the AI Engineer of the next decade.

## The Vibe Coding Manifesto

### 1. Intent Over Syntax
I will focus on *what* needs to be built, not *how* to type it. I will treat language as the highest-level programming interface.

### 2. Trust But Verify
I will expect the AI to be brilliant and lazy. I will never deploy code I have not read. I will never execute a plan I have not reviewed. The "Enter" key is my signature, and I am responsible for the result.

### 3. Systems Over Snips
I will not just generate snippets; I will architect systems. I will build contexts using RAG, I will build resilience using Agents, and I will build safety using Sandboxes.

### 4. Continuous Curiosity
I will not let my knowledge atrophy. I will use the time saved by AI to dive deeper into the fundamentals of Distributed Systems, Cryptography, and UX. I will remain an Engineer, not just an Operator.

### 5. Humanity in the Loop
I recognize that software is for people. I will use AI to amplify human creativity, not to replace human judgment.
