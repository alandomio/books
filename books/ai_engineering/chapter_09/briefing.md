# Chapter 9 Briefing: Frameworks and Tools

## Theme
**The Battle of the Frameworks.**
We have the patterns (Chapter 8). Now we pick the weapons. This chapter is a hands-on, code-first tour of the Big Three: LangGraph, CrewAI, and AutoGen. (Mention Agno briefly but focus on the top 3).

## Plot Points (The Narrative Arc)

### Scene A: LangGraph (The Graph)
*   **Philosophy:** Explicit control flow. State Machines.
*   **Key Feature:** `StateGraph`, `checkpointing`, cycles.
*   **Vibe:** Industrial, precise, "hard engineering."

### Scene B: CrewAI (The Team)
*   **Philosophy:** Role-based. Managers and Workers.
*   **Key Feature:** `Agent`, `Task`, `Crew`, `Process.sequential`.
*   **Vibe:** Corporate, structured, "business logic."

### Scene C: AutoGen (The Conversation)
*   **Philosophy:** Multi-agent Chat.
*   **Key Feature:** `UserProxyAgent`, `AssistantAgent`, `human_input_mode`.
*   **Vibe:** Academic, experimental, "collaborative."

### Scene D: The Comparison (Choosing Your Stack)
*   **Matrix:** A comparative analysis.
*   **Factors:** Cost, Latency, Reliability, Complexity.
*   **Recommendation:** When to use what.

## Tone & Style
*   **Comparative:** Constantly contrast the tools. "Unlike CrewAI, LangGraph forces you to..."
*   **Code-Centric:** Show "Hello World" for each.
*   **Objective:** Don't sell; evaluate.
