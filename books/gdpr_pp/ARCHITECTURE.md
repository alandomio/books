# Book Proposal: *Compliance as a Product*

**Subtitle:** *Scaling IoT Innovation in the Shadow of NIS2 and GDPR*

## 1. Value Proposition for the Agent

Most security books are written for engineers. This book is for the **Product Leadership**—the people who decide *what* to build. It teaches them how to treat compliance as a "First-Class Citizen" in the product backlog. Using the **Peter Park** ecosystem, it illustrates how a product can be both aggressive in its growth and invisible in its data footprint.

## 2. Updated Chapter Plan

### Part I: The Product Vision & Legal Framework

* **Chapter 1: The "Invisible Barrier" Strategy.** How to pitch a product that replaces physical infrastructure with digital "FreeFlow." Balancing user experience (UX) with the surveillance reality of ANPR.
* **Chapter 2: The Dual-Brand Product Architecture.** Why POs must care about corporate structure. Using **MHP** as a "Risk-Buffer" to keep the core **Peter Park** SaaS platform lean and attractive to B2B tech partners.

### Part II: Building the Compliant Backlog

* **Chapter 3: Privacy as a Feature.** Shifting from "How do we store this data?" to "How do we delete it faster?"
* **Chapter 4: The 48-Hour Deletion Logic.** A masterclass for POs on "Data Minimization." How to design systems that self-purge, reducing liability and infrastructure costs simultaneously.
* **Chapter 5: Identity & Multi-Tenancy.** Managing the complex hierarchy of B2B (Municipalities), B2B2C (Parking App users), and Internal Admin roles via Auth0.

### Part III: Technical Excellence for Product Leaders

* **Chapter 6: NIS2: The New "Definition of Done."** Explaining the Cyber Resilience Act and NIS2 to non-technical stakeholders. Why a "Resilience Budget" is now mandatory for every product roadmap.
* **Chapter 7: Payment UX vs. PCI Compliance.** Balancing the friction-less "Post-Payment" via the Bazar service with the rigid security requirements of card processors.
* **Chapter 8: IoT Fleet Management.** The logistics of managing 1,000+ cameras and kiosks. How the Head of Product manages hardware lifecycles and "secure-by-default" provisioning.

### Part IV: Operations & Growth

* **Chapter 9: The Automated Enforcement Pipeline.** How Product Owners design the "Halterdaten" (PII) interface with the KBA to ensure maximum efficiency for the MHP subsidiary.
* **Chapter 10: Scaling to Municipalities.** Selling to the public sector ("Kommunen"). How to use your Security Helpbook as a primary sales tool to win government contracts.
